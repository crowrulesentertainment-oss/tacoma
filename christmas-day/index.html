THE MAGIC OF DECEMBER 25

Let the day unfold
one moment at a time.

Morning light on the tree. The sound of wrapping paper. A favorite meal. A call from someone far away. Christmas Day is made of moments, big and small — and every household has its own way of making them meaningful.

However you spend the day, there is room for you in this celebration.

A LITTLE SOMETHING FOR EVERYONE

MAKE IT YOUR CHRISTMAS

Different traditions.
One shared spirit.

There is no single right way to celebrate. Find the moments that feel like yours.

01 / CONNECT
✧

Bring people closer

Call someone, visit a neighbor, send a message or make time for the people you haven't seen in a while.

02 / GATHER
❋

Share a favorite meal, a sweet treat, a recipe passed down or whatever makes the table feel welcoming.

03 / REMEMBER
✦

Keep the memories

Honor traditions, tell old stories, remember loved ones and make space for new beginnings.
