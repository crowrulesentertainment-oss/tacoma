Plain-language privacy

Privacy notice.

What the community hub collects, what may become public, and how to protect your information.

Information you submit

When you send a listing, resource, event, or community idea, the information is stored for moderation. Optional contact details are provided for follow-up and are not intended to appear in public listing cards. Do not submit information you would not want the moderation team to receive.

Publication and moderation

Submissions are not published automatically. Approved listing details may be visible to anyone on the internet. Avoid sensitive personal details, private home addresses, confidential records, and information about other people that you do not have permission to share.

Saved links

The Community Dashboard can store saved links in your browser's local storage. These saved links stay on that device and are not sent to the Hub's database by this feature. Clearing browser site data may remove them.

Service providers

The Hub is hosted through GitHub Pages and uses Supabase for published content and submission processing. Weather information and map searches may involve external providers such as Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap. Existing pages may load other third-party resources. Those providers operate under their own policies.

Cookies and tracking

The new community tools do not add advertising trackers or analytics pixels. Browser behavior, network logs, and essential platform processing may still be handled by the services that host and deliver the site.

Retention and requests

Submissions may be retained for moderation, safety, and correction history. To request a correction or discuss information you submitted, use the site's contact or correction pathway. Do not include passwords or confidential documents.

Not legal advice

This notice is a plain-language starting point, not a guarantee of legal compliance or a substitute for legal advice. Review it and adapt it to the actual services and data practices before promoting the site broadly.
