Our community commitment

People first. Always.

Tacoma Community Hub is being developed as a free community information and storytelling initiative of CrowRules Entertainment. We are starting small and growing at a pace our available resources allow.

✓Free access to the hub and its public information.
✓No donations or payments collected through this site.
✓Links to existing organizations remain under their control.
✓Community submissions require appropriate permission.
✓Respectful participation and community moderation.
✓Honest communication about what we can and cannot provide.

Our no-money policy

Tacoma Community Hub does not solicit or accept donations, distribute financial assistance, or promise grants or benefits. No payment is required to access the site. If you choose to support an external organization, you deal directly with that organization under its own policies.

Privacy and submissions

Do not submit passwords, financial account information, medical records, or sensitive personal details. Before this site accepts real submissions, CrowRules must configure a secure submission channel and publish a complete privacy notice explaining collection, use, retention, and deletion.

Our role

We share community information and connect visitors to existing providers. We are not a government agency, charitable organization, emergency service, or substitute for professional advice. Provider links do not imply endorsement.
