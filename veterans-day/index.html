NOVEMBER 11 · A DAY OF RECOGNITION

VETERANS DAY
IN TACOMA

A moment to recognize the people who have served in the United States Armed Forces, remember the sacrifices made, and support veterans and their families in our community.

NOVEMBER 11 · EVERY YEAR

HONOR SERVICE · SUPPORT OUR COMMUNITY

Remember. Recognize. Stand together.

Veterans Day is observed each November 11. It is an opportunity to express appreciation to those who served, listen to veterans' experiences when they choose to share them, and recognize the families and communities who stand alongside them.

01 / RECOGNIZE

Say thank you

Offer a sincere message of appreciation, attend a public remembrance, or recognize a veteran in your family or neighborhood—with their permission.

02 / LISTEN

Make space for stories

Every veteran's experience is different. Listen respectfully, avoid assumptions, and let each person decide what they want to share.

03 / SUPPORT

Help beyond one day

Learn about established local organizations supporting veterans, service members and their families. Confirm current needs directly with each organization.

Community information: This page is an independent Tacoma Community Hub tribute, not an official government or veterans-services resource. Event details and support services should be confirmed directly with their organizers or providers.
