NOVEMBER 11 · A DAY OF RECOGNITION

VETERANS DAY
IN TACOMA

A moment to recognize the people who have served in the United States Armed Forces, remember the sacrifices made, and support veterans and their families in our community.

NOVEMBER 11 · EVERY YEAR

HONOR SERVICE · SUPPORT OUR COMMUNITY

Remember. Recognize. Stand together.

Veterans Day is observed each November 11. It is an opportunity to express appreciation to those who served, listen to veterans' experiences when they choose to share them, and recognize the families and communities who stand alongside them.

01 / RECOGNIZE

Say thank you

Offer a sincere message of appreciation, attend a public remembrance, or recognize a veteran in your family or neighborhood—with their permission.

02 / LISTEN

Make space for stories

Every veteran's experience is different. Listen respectfully, avoid assumptions, and let each person decide what they want to share.

03 / SUPPORT

Help beyond one day

Learn about established local organizations supporting veterans, service members and their families. Confirm current needs directly with each organization.

SMALL GESTURES · REAL APPRECIATION

Ways to say “Thank you.”

You don't need a grand gesture or a donation to show gratitude. Thoughtful, respectful actions—on Veterans Day and throughout the year—can mean a great deal.

01 / IN PERSON

Offer a genuine thank-you

If a veteran is comfortable being recognized, keep it simple: “Thank you for your service. I appreciate what you did.” Let them decide whether they want to talk more.

02 / A PERSONAL MESSAGE

Write a note or make a call

Send a card, text, email or phone call to a veteran you know. Make it personal, mention something you appreciate, and remember military families and caregivers too.

03 / SHOW UP

Attend a remembrance

Look for public Veterans Day ceremonies, memorial services and community gatherings in Tacoma and Pierce County. Check the organizer's latest details before attending.

04 / KEEP LISTENING

Make room for their story

Ask whether they would like to share their experience. Listen without pressing for details, debating their service, or making assumptions about what they went through.

05 / EVERYDAY SUPPORT

Offer practical help

Ask before helping with errands, transportation, a meal, yard work or a household task. Respect independence, privacy and the answer they give.

06 / SHOP WITH PURPOSE

Support veteran-owned businesses

When a local business identifies itself as veteran-owned, consider learning about it, sharing its work or becoming a customer if it fits your needs and budget.

A MESSAGE YOU CAN SHARE

“Today, we recognize and thank the veterans in our community. Your service and your experiences matter. We appreciate you—not just today, but throughout the year. To veterans and military families across Tacoma and the South Sound: thank you.”

TURN APPRECIATION INTO ACTION

Give your time. Offer support.

If you'd like to do more, connect with established organizations and ask what is genuinely useful right now. Needs, volunteer roles and donation rules change, so always confirm before giving money, goods or time.

TACOMA & PIERCE COUNTY

Local veteran connections.

Sometimes the best way to support veterans is to help connect them—or their families—with the right local organization. These official resources can help people find services and learn where assistance is available.

Pierce County Veterans Services

Veterans Resource Center, 1501 Market Street, Suite 170, Tacoma · 253-798-7449. County assistance includes help with benefits claims and referrals for emergency financial, food, housing, transportation and utility support.

Urgent support for a veteran in crisis

Call 988 and press 1, or text 838255 to reach the Veterans Crisis Line. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.
