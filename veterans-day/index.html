A DAY OF GRATITUDE & REMEMBRANCE

We remember the people behind the uniform.

Veterans Day in Tacoma is a community tribute created by CrowRules Entertainment for Tacoma and the South Sound. It is an independent annual initiative—not an official City of Tacoma, Department of Defense or veterans-service organization event.

Our aim is to create a respectful place where neighbors can recognize veterans, listen to their experiences, appreciate military families and learn about the many ways people continue to serve after their time in uniform.

01 / RECOGNITION

Honor every generation

Recognize veterans from every branch, era and walk of life, while respecting each person's own story.

02 / CONNECTION

Listen with respect

Make room for veterans and families to share only what they choose, in their own words.

03 / COMMUNITY

Turn gratitude into action

Learn about local support, volunteer opportunities and practical ways to stand alongside veterans.

SMALL ACTS. LASTING RESPECT.

Ways to honor veterans.

There is no single right way to observe Veterans Day. Choose a thoughtful action that is appropriate, welcomed and within your means.

01 · SAY THANK YOU

Offer sincere appreciation

A simple, genuine thank-you can matter. Let the veteran decide whether they want to talk about their service.

02 · LISTEN

Make space for stories

Listen without pressing for details. Some experiences are personal, difficult or not meant to be shared publicly.

03 · LEARN

Understand the day

Learn about Veterans Day, the different branches of service and the experiences of veterans in our region.

04 · SUPPORT

Find trusted local services

Share verified information from established veterans' organizations and government agencies.

05 · INCLUDE

Remember military families

Recognize spouses, children, caregivers and loved ones whose lives are also shaped by military service.

06 · KEEP IT GOING

Serve your neighborhood

Volunteer, check on a neighbor or contribute time to a local cause throughout the year.

BEYOND THE DAY OF SERVICE

Recognizing military families.

Service affects more than the person in uniform. Families navigate deployments, moves, uncertainty, reunions, caregiving and transitions. Their experiences deserve recognition without assuming every family has the same needs.

Respect each family's story

Ask what support would be useful rather than making assumptions.

Offer practical help

When welcomed, offer a meal, a ride, childcare support or help with an everyday task.

Stay connected

Remember that support and friendship matter after ceremonies and commemorative dates have passed.

OUR PERMANENT COMMUNITY PROMISE

Free means free.

There are no participation fees, required purchases, paid memberships or required donations to take part in this online community tribute. Contributions are optional. No veteran or family member owes anyone a story, photograph, appearance or public acknowledgement.

Veterans Day in Tacoma is an independent community initiative created by CrowRules Entertainment and supported by Tacoma Community Hub. It is not an official government event and does not claim endorsement by any military branch or veterans agency.
