TACOMA BUSINESS OUTREACH • 2026

Let’s make Thanksgiving
bigger than all of us.

Invite Tacoma and South Sound businesses to take part in a free, community-led celebration of gratitude, local connection and neighbor-to-neighbor kindness.

Invitation email

Suggested subject: Tacoma businesses: join Thanksgiving in Tacoma 2026

Hello, I’m reaching out on behalf of Tacoma Community Hub to invite [Business Name] to participate in Thanksgiving in Tacoma 2026 — “A City. A Community. A Thousand Reasons to Be Thankful.” This community initiative is bringing Tacoma neighbors together through stories of gratitude, acts of kindness, local activities, a community recipe exchange and opportunities to recognize the people and organizations that make our city special. We’d love to include your business. Participation can be as simple as sharing your story and receiving a free community partner listing. You may also suggest a free community activity, volunteer opportunity, neighborhood gathering or an optional in-kind contribution. There is no requirement to provide money, products, discounts or services. There is no sponsorship fee, purchase, donation or paid promotion required. Any optional contribution is entirely your choice. We’ll review submissions and confirm any public listing or feature with you before publishing. Participation does not guarantee interview or broadcast coverage. Learn more or submit your interest: https://crowrulesentertainment-oss.github.io/tacoma/thanksgiving/#partners Thank you for considering being part of a positive Thanksgiving season for Tacoma. Warm regards, Tacoma Community Hub CrowRules Entertainment

Personalize the business name and your own signature before sending. Send individually and respect any request not to be contacted again.

Outreach guidelines

  • Start with locally owned businesses, neighborhood shops, restaurants, service providers, creative studios and local employers.
  • Ask permission before using logos, photos, quotes or business names in promotional materials.
  • Never imply a donation, discount or product contribution is expected.
  • Confirm details before publishing a listing or arranging a media appearance.
  • Record the contact date, response and agreed follow-up in the private admin tracker.
