NO PURCHASE. NO DONATION. JUST KINDNESS.

253 Acts of Kindness

Make someone's day a little brighter. Choose an act that is safe, comfortable and within your means. You never need to spend money to take part.

Check in

Call, text or visit a neighbor who might appreciate a friendly hello.

Share your time

Offer to help with a small task, carry groceries or explain something useful.

Say thank you

Write a note to someone whose work often goes unnoticed.

Share a skill

Teach a neighbor a recipe, repair tip, craft or practical skill.

Include someone

Invite a person who may be spending the holiday alone to join a public, welcoming activity.

Care for a shared place

Join an organized neighborhood cleanup or help keep a shared space welcoming.

Tell Tacoma about your act of kindness

Share a short story without identifying anyone who has not agreed to be named. Your story will be reviewed before appearing publicly.

Share an act of kindness

FREE ACTIVITIES FOR ALL AGES

Thanksgiving Family Fun

Use your browser print dialog to save a free PDF.

Gratitude bingo

Make a five-by-five bingo grid with moments of kindness, connection and thankfulness.

Conversation cards

Ask: What is a Tacoma place you love? Who taught you something important? What tradition would you pass on?

Neighborhood photo hunt

Find autumn colors, a welcoming public space, a local landmark and something that makes you smile. Respect private property.

Thankful leaf craft

Draw or cut out paper leaves and write one thing you appreciate on each.

Recipe memory card

Record a favorite dish, who made it, and the memory connected to it.

Kindness calendar

Choose one small, free act of kindness for each day of Thanksgiving week.

Activities are optional and designed to be adapted for different ages, abilities, sensory needs and family traditions. Children should participate with a responsible adult.

OUR PERMANENT COMMUNITY PROMISE

Free means free.

No participation fees

No tickets, dues, paid memberships or required purchases to take part in the digital campaign.

No donation requirement

Giving is always optional. Kindness, stories and time are equally valued.

No paywall

Public campaign information and approved community stories remain freely accessible.

Some independently organized partner events may have their own conditions. Every listing must disclose whether it is free, requires registration, has limited capacity or involves an optional contribution. Tacoma Community Hub does not promise food, transportation or supplies unless a confirmed partner has arranged them.
