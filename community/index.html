How the Hub keeps information responsible

Published information may be incomplete or out of date. The Hub distinguishes publication from independent verification, provides correction pathways, and links to official sources when available. Do not rely on community listings alone for emergencies, legal deadlines, medical decisions, or other high-stakes matters.
