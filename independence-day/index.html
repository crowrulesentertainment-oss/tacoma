A NEW EVENT · A FIRST CHAPTER

We’re starting something new in Tacoma.

Independence Day in Tacoma is not a long-running festival or an established city tradition. It is a brand-new annual community event being created and organized by CrowRules Entertainment, an independent entertainment company based in Tacoma and being built from the ground up.

The 2027 campaign is the first edition—the first step in creating a free, welcoming celebration that can return every July, grow with community participation and build a lasting archive of family stories and tributes. Tacoma Community Hub is the community platform for this initiative; CrowRules Entertainment is the company developing and producing it.
