Trust through transparency.

We want people to understand what a listing’s labels mean—and what they do not mean. We distinguish publication moderation from actual source verification, and we only display a review date when one has been recorded.

Important: A published listing is not automatically verified, endorsed, or guaranteed to be current. Please contact the organization directly before relying on time-sensitive details.

What the labels mean

Each public listing may show one of the following statuses, based on the latest status recorded by a Hub administrator.

✓ Verified by source

Source confirmation recorded

An administrator has checked a credible source for the listing and recorded this status. It is not a certification, endorsement, guarantee of service quality, or promise that details cannot change.

Details checked

Basic details reviewed

An administrator recorded a details check, but has not marked the listing as verified by source. Do not interpret this as independent confirmation.

Not verified

No verification status recorded

The Hub has not recorded source verification for this listing. The listing may still be published, but its information should be confirmed directly with the provider.

Needs review

Follow-up needed

The listing has been flagged for additional attention. Information may be incomplete, unclear or potentially outdated.

How a listing gets published

  • Community members or administrators provide a listing.
  • A moderator reviews submissions before deciding whether to publish them.
  • Publication moderation helps keep the directory appropriate; it does not by itself verify the provider or confirm every claim.
  • When an administrator checks a source, they can record a verification status and the date reviewed.
  • Public reports about errors or outdated details are routed to the administrative review queue.

Review dates and freshness

“Last reviewed” means a moderator recorded a specific review date. If no date is displayed, the Hub has no recorded review date for that listing. A general “listing updated” or “published” date is not the same thing as a review date.

Listings can change after review. For opening hours, eligibility, prices, service availability, event details, and urgent needs, confirm directly with the organization or official source.

Suggest an edit

Anyone can suggest a corrected title, description, organization name, website, or location from a listing in Discover. Suggestions are sent privately to the Hub review queue; they do not change the published listing automatically. An administrator reviews the proposal and updates the listing if appropriate.

Public correction history

When a moderator has made and checked a meaningful correction, the Hub may publish a short dated note on the listing. Only notes explicitly marked public by an administrator appear in the public history. The history does not expose the original report, submitter identity, private review notes, or unapproved suggestions.

Report a problem

If you find an outdated phone number, broken link, duplicate entry, closed service, or other issue, use “Report outdated information” on the listing in Discover. Reports are reviewed by administrators; submitting a report does not automatically change or remove a listing.
