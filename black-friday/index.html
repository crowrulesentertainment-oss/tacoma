A LOCAL TAKE ON BLACK FRIDAY

Make room for the businesses that make Tacoma.

Black Friday in Tacoma is an independent, free community guide created by CrowRules Entertainment. It is designed to help residents discover local businesses and community happenings during the holiday shopping season—not to pressure anyone to spend.

We welcome independent retailers, restaurants, makers, artists, service providers, markets and neighborhood organizations to share accurate public information. Listings and promotions are supplied by participating businesses and are not endorsements or guarantees by CrowRules Entertainment.

A LITTLE PLANNING GOES A LONG WAY

Shop smart. Stay in control.

01 · PLAN

Set a comfortable budget

Decide what you need and what you can comfortably spend before browsing promotions.

02 · COMPARE

Check the real price

Compare total costs, shipping, return policies and product specifications—not just the advertised discount.

03 · VERIFY

Read the fine print

Confirm dates, stock, exclusions, eligibility, warranties and refund conditions with the seller.

04 · PROTECT

Use secure checkout

Shop through trusted websites, use secure payment methods and avoid suspicious links or payment requests.

05 · PAUSE

Beware of pressure tactics

Countdowns, urgency messages and unusually large discounts are not proof that a deal is genuine.

06 · REMEMBER

It's okay not to buy

The best deal may be keeping your money. Participation in this guide never requires a purchase.
