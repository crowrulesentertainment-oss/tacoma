Production notes

  • Camera and microphone tests run in the host’s browser after permission is granted.
  • Guest video uses a direct browser WebRTC connection with public STUN assistance. Some networks require a TURN relay; this first version does not include a paid TURN service.
  • Invitation records and producer queue are stored in the producer’s browser. They are not yet a shared, server-persisted invitation database; keep the producer browser open during the session.
  • Use OBS Studio for the currently supported remote scene switching. Streamlabs Desktop needs its own adapter.
