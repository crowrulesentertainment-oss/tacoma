CROWRULES ENTERTAINMENT • ANNUAL COMMUNITY EVENT • 2026–2027

Let’s bring Tacoma
into the new year together.

24 Hours of Tacoma is a new annual New Year's Eve community event created and produced by CrowRules Entertainment, which operates Tacoma Community Hub, with Tacoma Nights. The 2026–2027 celebration is the inaugural edition: a continuous 24-hour community broadcast beginning December 31, 2026 at noon Pacific and continuing through January 1, 2027 at noon Pacific.

CrowRules Entertainment is an independent entertainment venture being built from the ground up by its founder and is currently operated by the founder as an individual. We are inviting Tacoma-area businesses, creators, nonprofits, neighborhood groups and community organizations to help shape the first year of an event intended to return annually.

Outreach guidelines

  • Start with Tacoma and Pierce County businesses, neighborhood organizations, nonprofits, schools, arts groups, creators and community leaders.
  • Describe this accurately as a new, independently produced event; 2026–2027 is its inaugural edition and it is intended to return annually.
  • Offer low-pressure choices: an approved listing, community message, short video greeting, interview interest or local feature.
  • No fee, purchase, donation, discount, product or in-kind contribution is expected or required.
  • Get explicit permission before using names, logos, photos, quotes, music, links or submitted media.
  • Confirm each participant's role, timing, format and publication permissions in writing.
  • Do not imply City of Tacoma sponsorship, endorsement, partnership or approval.
  • Do not promise a feature, interview, audience size or broadcast placement.
  • Keep private contact details secure and record opt-outs so they are not contacted again.
  • For commercial email, use truthful sender and subject information, include a valid physical postal address and a clear opt-out method, and promptly honor opt-out requests.
  • Track contact date, response, permissions and follow-up in the private Event Control Room.
