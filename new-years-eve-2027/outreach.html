CROWRULES ENTERTAINMENT • ANNUAL COMMUNITY EVENT • 2026–2027

Let’s bring Tacoma
into the new year together.

24 Hours of Tacoma is a brand-new annual New Year's Eve community event created and produced by CrowRules Entertainment with Tacoma Community Hub and Tacoma Nights. The 2026–2027 celebration is the inaugural edition: a continuous 24-hour community broadcast beginning December 31, 2026 at noon Pacific and continuing through January 1, 2027 at noon Pacific.

We are inviting Tacoma-area businesses, creators, nonprofits, neighborhood groups and community organizations to help shape the first year of an event intended to return annually.

Invitation email

Suggested subject: Invitation to be part of 24 Hours of Tacoma — an annual community event

Hello, I’m reaching out on behalf of CrowRules Entertainment and Tacoma Community Hub to invite [Business / Organization Name] to be part of 24 Hours of Tacoma, our inaugural New Year's Eve community event. 24 Hours of Tacoma is a new annual event being created from the ground up by CrowRules Entertainment, an independent Tacoma entertainment company. The first edition will be a 24-hour community broadcast from December 31, 2026 at noon through January 1, 2027 at noon Pacific, bringing together local stories, creators, businesses, community organizations, entertainment and a live midnight celebration. We would love to feature your organization or invite you to participate. Ideas include a short video greeting, an interview or community spotlight, a local story, a New Year's message, a community activity, or an optional in-kind contribution. Participation can also be as simple as allowing us to share an approved description and link to your organization. There is no sponsorship fee, purchase, donation, discount or product contribution required. Any optional contribution is entirely your choice. We will confirm details and obtain permission before publishing names, logos, photos, quotes or other materials. An invitation or submission does not guarantee selection, a feature, or on-air coverage. Learn about the event and express interest: https://crowrulesentertainment-oss.github.io/tacoma/new-years-eve-2027/ This is the first year of a community event we intend to bring back annually. Early participating businesses and organizations can help shape the event's foundation, with no financial commitment required. Thank you for considering being part of 24 Hours of Tacoma. Warm regards, [Your Name] CrowRules Entertainment Tacoma Community Hub × Tacoma Nights

Personalize the recipient, organization name and signature before sending. Contact organizations individually and respect any request not to be contacted again.

Outreach guidelines

  • Begin with Tacoma and Pierce County businesses, neighborhood organizations, nonprofits, schools, arts groups, creators and community leaders.
  • Explain that this is a new, independently produced annual event; 2026–2027 is its inaugural edition.
  • Offer clear, low-pressure participation choices: community message, short video greeting, interview interest, local feature or approved resource listing.
  • No fee, purchase, donation, discount or in-kind contribution is expected. Never imply otherwise.
  • Get permission before using names, logos, photos, quotes, music or submitted media.
  • Confirm the details, timing and format with each participant before scheduling or publishing.
  • Track contact date, response, permission and follow-up in the private Event Control Room.
  • Participation does not guarantee selection, placement or broadcast coverage.
