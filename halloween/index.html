A TACOMA HALLOWEEN TRADITION

Make spooky season a little more local.

Halloween in Tacoma is an independent, free community celebration created by CrowRules Entertainment. It's a place to find local activities, discover creative neighbors and enjoy the season together—from pumpkin patches and costume gatherings to neighborhood events and autumn art.

Not every celebration needs to be scary, expensive or late at night. We want this page to help people find the experiences that feel right for them.

A SAFE & WELCOMING CELEBRATION

Keep the fun in Halloween.

01 · BE VISIBLE

See and be seen

Use reflective details or lights, stay on sidewalks where available and cross at visible intersections.

02 · STAY TOGETHER

Make a simple plan

Choose a route, agree on a check-in plan and keep children with a trusted adult as appropriate.

03 · CHECK TREATS

Review before enjoying

For young children, inspect treats and avoid anything opened, damaged or unfamiliar.

04 · RESPECT OTHERS

Honor boundaries

Respect signs, property, quiet hours and neighbors who choose not to participate.

05 · INCLUDE

Make celebrations accessible

Offer non-food treats where possible and be considerate of sensory needs and mobility access.

06 · PLAN AHEAD

Know your event

Confirm hours, parking, costs, weather plans and age recommendations with organizers.

These are general reminders, not a substitute for local safety guidance or event-specific instructions.
