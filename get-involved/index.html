A thoughtful approach

How contributions should work

Good community participation is transparent, respectful, and realistic about what a small project can currently deliver.

Choose a focus

Decide what interests you, how much time you can offer, and what kind of contribution feels manageable.

Start with reliable information

Use official sources where possible. Clearly distinguish verified facts, personal opinions, and suggestions.

Respect people and permissions

Get appropriate consent before publishing someone's story, image, recording, or other personal contribution.

Build at a sustainable pace

Focus on achievable improvements. Don't promise services, partnerships, or publication dates that aren't confirmed.

Clear expectations

Participation with transparency

The project should be honest about its current capacity and about what it asks of anyone who chooses to help.

Community first

No pay-to-be-listed requirement

The Community Hub is intended to help people discover useful local information. This page does not offer paid placement, paid membership, or a subscription requirement for participation.

Volunteer expectations

Volunteer work must be understood clearly

CrowRules is being built from the ground up with limited resources. Any volunteer opportunity must clearly state its scope and expectations. There is no promise of payment, future employment, or future compensation.

Accuracy & trust

Verify before publishing

Local services, eligibility rules, event dates, contact information, and opening hours can change. Entries should be checked against reliable sources and corrected when they become outdated.

Safety & respect

Protect privacy and dignity

Don't submit private information, confidential records, or another person's contact details without permission. Stories about sensitive situations require particular care, consent, and safeguards.
