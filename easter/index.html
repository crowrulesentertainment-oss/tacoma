FREE IDEAS FOR ALL AGES

Make a little springtime magic.

Simple activities to enjoy at home, outdoors or with your community. Adapt each idea to different ages, abilities and sensory needs.

01

Spring color hunt

Find five colors in nature, photograph them and make a little spring collage. Leave plants and wildlife undisturbed.

02

Decorate an egg

Use paper, washable colors or recycled materials to create a one-of-a-kind Easter design.

03

Family story cards

Ask: What does a fresh start mean to you? What is a favorite spring memory? What would you like to learn this year?

04

Make a kindness bouquet

Write kind words or helpful actions on paper flowers and share them with people you appreciate.

05

Neighborhood photo safari

Look for spring colors, public art, local landmarks and signs of the season. Respect private property and people's privacy.

06

Build a spring playlist

Invite everyone to suggest a song that makes them feel hopeful, joyful or ready for a new beginning.

Children should take part with a responsible adult. Activities are optional, free and designed to be adapted for your household.
