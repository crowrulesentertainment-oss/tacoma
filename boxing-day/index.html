A TACOMA BOXING DAY

Carry the spirit of giving forward.

Boxing Day is observed on December 26 in many countries and is associated with generosity, sharing and time with family and community. Boxing Day in Tacoma is an independent, free community guide from CrowRules Entertainment, highlighting ways to connect and take part across the South Sound.

Traditions vary between households and cultures. Whether you spend the day volunteering, visiting loved ones, enjoying a winter walk, supporting a neighborhood business or simply resting, there's no single right way to celebrate.

MORE THAN A DATE ON THE CALENDAR
