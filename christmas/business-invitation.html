THE FIRST CHAPTER STARTS HERE

A new annual community initiative—built from the ground up.

Christmas Together — Tacoma 2026 is the inaugural edition of a new annual community event created and organized by CrowRules Entertainment, an independent Tacoma entertainment company being built from the ground up.

This is not an established festival or a City of Tacoma program. We are starting with the first edition in 2026, inviting local residents, organizations and businesses to help shape an event we hope to bring back each year. Tacoma Community Hub is the free digital community platform supporting the initiative; CrowRules Entertainment is its creator and producer.

WHY WE’RE REACHING OUT

We want local businesses in the story from the beginning.

Local businesses help give Tacoma its character. Our goal is to create a welcoming place where residents can discover local shops and services, hear the stories behind them, and find ways to support the community throughout the holiday season.

01 / BE DISCOVERED

Free community listing

Introduce your business, share your website and tell the community what you offer. Approved listings may appear in the Christmas Together local business directory.

02 / SHARE YOUR STORY

Business spotlight

Tell us how your business began, what you do, and what makes your place in Tacoma special. You may express interest in a future interview or feature.

03 / MAKE A DIFFERENCE

Optional community activity

Suggest a free activity, kindness idea, volunteer opportunity or optional in-kind contribution. There is no expectation to donate or provide anything.

04 / JOIN THE CONVERSATION

OUR PROMISE TO LOCAL BUSINESSES

Participation is free.

No sponsorship fee. No advertising purchase. No required donation. No required discount. No required product or service contribution. Simply introducing your business is enough.

Submitting an interest form does not guarantee publication, promotion, an interview or a particular audience reach. Listings and submissions are reviewed before publication. Any optional activity or feature will be discussed and confirmed separately.

TAKE THE FIRST STEP

Your contact details are for follow-up and will not be displayed publicly. A directory listing is optional and requires separate review. This form is an expression of interest, not a contract or sponsorship agreement.
