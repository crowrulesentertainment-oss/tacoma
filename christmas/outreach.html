Responsible outreach checklist

  • Contact businesses individually with a truthful subject line and accurate sender information.
  • Identify yourself as an individual operating the CrowRules Entertainment venture; do not imply that it is incorporated or a nonprofit.
  • Include a valid physical mailing address and a clear opt-out method in commercial email.
  • Honor opt-out requests promptly and maintain a do-not-contact list.
  • Do not imply City of Tacoma sponsorship, approval, partnership or endorsement.
  • Do not describe participation as a charitable donation or tax-deductible contribution.
  • Get permission before publishing a business name, logo, photo, quote or contact details.
  • Confirm activities, listings and media arrangements in writing before announcing them.
  • Keep private contact details in the restricted admin tracker, not on public pages.
