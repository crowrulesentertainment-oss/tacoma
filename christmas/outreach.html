Christmas Together — Tacoma 2026 is a brand-new annual community event created and organized by CrowRules Entertainment, an independent Tacoma entertainment company being built from the ground up. The 2026 edition is its inaugural year. We are inviting local businesses to help shape the first chapter of an event we intend to bring back each Christmas season.

Invitation email

Suggested subject: Tacoma businesses: join Christmas Together — Tacoma 2026

Hello, I’m reaching out on behalf of Tacoma Community Hub to invite [Business Name] to participate in Christmas Together — Tacoma 2026 — “One Community. One Christmas. Countless Stories.” This inaugural edition will bring neighbors together through Christmas stories and memories, holiday lights, local business discoveries, acts of kindness, community activities and opportunities to recognize the people and organizations that make our city special. We’d love to include your business. Participation can be as simple as sharing your story and receiving a free community partner listing. You may also suggest a free community activity, volunteer opportunity, neighborhood gathering or an optional in-kind contribution. There is no requirement to provide money, products, discounts or services. There is no sponsorship fee, purchase, donation or paid promotion required. Any optional contribution is entirely your choice. We’ll review submissions and confirm any public listing or feature with you before publishing. Participation does not guarantee interview or broadcast coverage. Learn more or submit your interest: https://crowrulesentertainment-oss.github.io/tacoma/christmas/business-invitation.html Thank you for considering being part of the first year of something new. Early founding business participants can help shape this annual community initiative, with no financial commitment required. Warm regards, Tacoma Community Hub CrowRules Entertainment

Personalize the business name and your own signature before sending. Send individually and respect any request not to be contacted again.

Outreach guidelines

  • Start with locally owned businesses, neighborhood shops, restaurants, service providers, creative studios and local employers.
  • Ask permission before using logos, photos, quotes or business names in promotional materials.
  • Never imply a donation, discount or product contribution is expected.
  • Confirm details before publishing a listing or arranging a media appearance.
  • Record the contact date, response and agreed follow-up in the private admin tracker.
