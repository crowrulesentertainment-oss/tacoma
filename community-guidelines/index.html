Community standards

Share useful information. Respect your neighbors.

Guidelines for business listings, community notices, public sources, events, and resident suggestions.

Submit information you can stand behind

  • Be accurate, specific, and respectful. Correct mistakes when you discover them.
  • Only submit information and images you created or have permission to share.
  • Do not post private information, confidential records, threats, harassment, or unsupported allegations about a person or organization.
  • For jobs, offers, and events, include dates and link to the official source where possible.
  • Do not present a listing as officially endorsed, licensed, or verified unless that status is documented.
  • Do not use the Hub to impersonate a business, manipulate reviews, spam, or mislead residents.

Moderation and corrections

Submissions remain pending until reviewed. Moderators may decline, edit for clarity, request more information, or remove material that conflicts with these standards. Published information can be reported or corrected through the available listing pathways.

Business listings and paid placements

Basic listings are intended to remain free. Any future paid promotion must be clearly labeled and must not imply a higher verification status. A listing's publication or owner claim does not itself mean the Hub independently verified every claim.

Safety and reliability

The Hub is not an emergency dispatch service. For immediate danger or urgent assistance, contact the appropriate official service. Verify important schedules, legal deadlines, service eligibility, and business details with the original provider.

Fairness and accessibility

We aim to make community information accessible, respectful, and useful across neighborhoods. Avoid discriminatory descriptions and share accessibility information when known. Do not claim accessibility features without checking them.
